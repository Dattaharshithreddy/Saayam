Saayam (సాయం) — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026 ·

Englishతెలుగు

Saayam is an independent guide app. It is not an official app of the Government of Andhra Pradesh or the Government of India and is not affiliated with any government department.

1. Who we are

Saayam explains Andhra Pradesh and central government schemes, certificates and processes in Telugu and English. Contact: dattaharshithreddy@gmail.com.

2. What we do not collect

  • No sign-up or account. We do not ask for your name, phone number, email, Aadhaar or location.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

3. Data that stays on your phone

Your language choice, saved guides, documents you tick in checklists, and the "bigger text" setting are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

4. The Saayam helper

The Saayam helper (the chat screen) works entirely on your phone. It answers from the guides stored inside the app. Nothing you type to it is sent to us or to anyone else, and it works without internet.

5. Reports and guide requests

If you tap "Report wrong information" or "Request a guide", the app opens your own email app with a message addressed to us. Nothing is sent until you press Send there. We receive the message and your email address, and use them only to correct or add guides and, if needed, to reply to you. We do not share them. Ask us to delete your message at any time by writing to dattaharshithreddy@gmail.com.

6. Read aloud and sharing

Read aloud uses your phone's own text-to-speech engine. Sharing uses your phone's share menu; we do not see what you share or to whom.

7. Children

Saayam is a general information app and is not directed at children under 13.

8. Deleting your data

Data on your phone is deleted when you uninstall the app. To ask us to delete a report you sent, email dattaharshithreddy@gmail.com with the approximate date and guide name.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
